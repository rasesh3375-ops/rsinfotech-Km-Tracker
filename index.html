Company letterhead

Printed in the header and footer of every generated report. Anything left blank is left off the report rather than filled with a placeholder.

Attendance

Marked Present as soon as the engineer takes a check-in selfie that day — whether or not they end up recording a trip. Sundays are excluded automatically; add festivals or declared holidays below so they don't count as absent either.

Holidays

Holiday List (to share with engineers)

A printable holiday list with the company logo, for a chosen financial year (April–March) — built from the Holidays list above.

EL Calculation Sheet

Earned Leave = 1 day per 25 days present, per the leave policy. Resident Engineers are excluded. This computes each employee's EL for the financial year you pick from the Attendance Sheet, and can carry the closing balance forward as next year's opening balance automatically.

Monthly Leave Report

Every employee's leave days (EL + SL + LP, half-days counted as 0.5) broken down month by month across the financial year — April through March — so you can see each person's full-year leave pattern at a glance.

PF Report

PF deducted per employee for the month, pulled from the Salary Sheet. Apprentices, R.S.IT Solution and Contractors don't have PF, so they're excluded here.

ESI Report

ESI deducted per employee for the month, pulled from the Salary Sheet. Apprentices, R.S.IT Solution and Contractors don't have ESI, so they're excluded here.

PT Report

Professional Tax deducted per employee for the month, pulled from the Salary Sheet. Apprentices and Contractors don't have PT — Managerial, Seniors, Junior Engineers, and R.S.IT Solution do, when Gross exceeds ₹12,000.

Apprentice Salary Report

Full salary breakdown for employees under the Apprentices heading only, for the month you pick — pulled from the Salary Sheet.

Consultant Report

The monthly attendance sheet in the exact column format the consultant asks for — one row per employee, built automatically from Employee Master and the month's attendance. Pick any month; export the CSV and send it on.

Month-wise Salary Comparison

Last month's total salary against the month you pick, and exactly what explains the gap — each new joiner that added to it, each leaver that took away from it, and anyone whose own salary moved. The reasons add up to the difference.

Overtime Report

Applies to Field Engineers per the overtime policy. Log hours HR has been informed of (by the following day, per policy) — this is manual since overtime is reported over WhatsApp, not through this app.
