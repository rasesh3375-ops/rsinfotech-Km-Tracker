Reports

View attendance, leave, payroll and employee reports — pick what you need below.

Attendance

Marked Present as soon as the engineer takes a check-in selfie that day — whether or not they end up recording a trip. Sundays are excluded automatically; add festivals or declared holidays below so they don't count as absent either.

Holidays

Monthly Leave Report

Every employee's leave days (EL + SL + LP, half-days counted as 0.5) broken down month by month across the financial year — April through March — so you can see each person's full-year leave pattern at a glance.

Monthly Leave Detail Report

Every employee's late coming, EL/PL, SL, half days and short leaves for one month, side by side. Whoever has the highest count in a column is shown in red, so it's easy to see at a glance who to have a word with.

Yearly Leave Detail Report

The same breakdown — late coming, EL/PL, SL, half days, short leaves — one table per category, month by month across the financial year with a Total column. The highest count in each column is shown in red.

PF Report

PF deducted per employee for the month, pulled from the Salary Sheet. Apprentices, R.S.IT Solution and Contractors don't have PF, so they're excluded here.

ESI Report

ESI deducted per employee for the month, pulled from the Salary Sheet. Apprentices, R.S.IT Solution and Contractors don't have ESI, so they're excluded here.

PT Report

Professional Tax deducted per employee for the month, pulled from the Salary Sheet. Apprentices and Contractors don't have PT — Managerial, Seniors, Junior Engineers, and R.S.IT Solution do, when Gross exceeds ₹12,000.

Apprentice Salary Report

Full salary breakdown for employees under the Apprentices heading only, for the month you pick — pulled from the Salary Sheet.

Final Salary Sheet for Accountant

The payment list: every employee grouped by salary heading, sorted by bank within each heading, with account number and the final amount payable after all deductions. Built for handing to the bank — one block per heading, subtotalled, with a grand total at the end.

Diwali Bonus Payment Sheet

The payment list for the bonus, in the same shape as the Final Salary Sheet — grouped by salary heading, sorted by bank, with account number and the amount payable. An employee appears once they are marked eligible and have a Diwali bonus amount set on their record; anyone eligible without one is named above the table rather than left off quietly. No PF, ESI, Professional Tax, advance, loan or retention is deducted, and leave taken during the year does not reduce it.

Consultant Report

The monthly attendance sheet in the exact column format the consultant asks for — one row per employee, built automatically from Employee Master and the month's attendance. Pick any month; export the CSV and send it on.

Month-wise Salary Comparison

Last month's total salary against the month you pick, and exactly what explains the gap — each new joiner that added to it, each leaver that took away from it, and anyone whose own salary moved. The reasons add up to the difference.

Overtime Report

Applies to Field Engineers per the overtime policy. Log hours HR has been informed of (by the following day, per policy) — this is manual since overtime is reported over WhatsApp, not through this app.

Sick Leave Balance Generator for New Year

Sets each employee's opening SL to the yearly allowance for their employee type. Resident Engineers and Work From Home staff are skipped, their policies being different, as are non-R.S. Infotech headings. Run once, in April. EL is not touched here — open it for the new year from the EL Calculation Sheet's "Encash and start next year at nil" or "Carry forward instead", so EL is only ever set from one place.

Generator

Generate HR reports, letters and calculation sheets — pick what you need below.

Employee Role Reports

A branded A4 report built from one employee's job profile, responsibilities, designation and department — the measures, risks and recommendations change with the role. Fill in Job profile and Key responsibilities in Employee Master first; without them the report falls back to designation and department and says so.

Company letterhead

Printed in the header and footer of every generated report. Anything left blank is left off the report rather than filled with a placeholder.

Whoever is picked here signs every generated letter and the "Approved by (HR)" line on Role Reports — their name, specimen signature and mobile number come straight from their own Employee Master record, so update it there, not here.

Holiday List (to share with engineers)

A printable holiday list with the company logo, for a chosen financial year (April–March) — built from the Holidays list above.

Leave Encashment Report

What is owed for unused privilege leave at the year end, as at 31 March. Grouped by salary heading and sorted by bank with account numbers, so it can be paid the same way as the salary and the bonus. Resident Engineers are outside the EL scheme and do not appear.

EL Calculation Sheet

Earned Leave = 1 day per 25 days present, per the leave policy. Resident Engineers are excluded. This computes each employee's EL for the financial year you pick from the Attendance Sheet, and can carry the closing balance forward as next year's opening balance automatically.
